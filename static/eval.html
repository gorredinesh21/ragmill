Eval — measured, not vibes

The hybrid-beats-dense claim is a number on this page and a test in CI (pytest tests/test_retrieval.py fails if RRF fusion ever loses to dense-only). The golden set: 60 auto-generated queries, each with one known-correct document.

hit@3 — of the queries, the fraction where the right document appears in the top 3 results. hit@10 — same, for the top 10: the practical "did the answer's evidence get retrieved at all" number. MRR (mean reciprocal rank) — how high the right document ranks, where 1.0 means always first and 0.5 means second on average. Higher is better everywhere.
