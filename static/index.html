Retrieval-augmented answering, built for scale

Ask questions about a large document collection — every answer comes with numbered sources you can check.

RAGMill runs two search engines at once — exact keywords and meaning — fuses their rankings, and lets an LLM answer from the best passages with [1][2] citations. Try it live on the bundled 5,000-paper research corpus: no signup, nothing to install.

What it is, in plain words

You type a question. Keyword search (BM25) and vector search (embeddings) each fetch passages; reciprocal rank fusion merges the two rankings; a reranker picks the best ones; the LLM writes a short answer citing [1][2] — click any number to jump to the exact source and verify the claim yourself.

Why hybrid search matters

Keyword search and vector search each miss things; fused, they beat both. Measured on a 60-query golden set over the 5K corpus:

0.72 hybrid hit@10 (dense-only 0.67)
0.36 hybrid MRR (dense-only 0.31)

Full breakdown — including the sparse-only column — lives on the Eval page, and it's a CI test, not a marketing sentence.

Built to scale

Ingestion is sharded across parallel Cloud Run Job tasks with deterministic, idempotent upserts — a crashed shard just re-runs and overwrites; nothing double-counts. The arithmetic puts a million documents within minutes of job time. This demo serves 5,000 docs on a scale-to-zero service for under $1/month.
